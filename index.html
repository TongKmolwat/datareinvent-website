About Us

Built Around One Idea: Data Only Creates Value When People Know How to Use It

Data Reinvent works alongside finance teams, analysts, operations leaders, and executives to close the gap between the tools organizations already own — Excel, Power BI, Microsoft Copilot — and the skills needed to use them with confidence.

We started from a simple observation: most organizations don't have a data problem, they have a data-fluency problem. Reports take too long to build, dashboards go unused, and new AI tools sit idle because no one has shown teams how they fit into real, everyday work. Our job is to fix that — through practical consulting and training built around your business, not generic courseware.

  • Practical over theoretical

    Every engagement uses real business scenarios and your own data, not abstract examples.

  • Business outcomes first

    We scope every project around a measurable result — time saved, clarity gained, decisions improved.

  • Built on the Microsoft ecosystem

    Deep, focused specialization in the tools most organizations already have licenses for.

  • Skills that stick

    We aim to leave your team more capable than we found them — not dependent on us.

Consulting Across the Tools You Already Rely On

Excel Consulting & Advisory

Power BI Solutions

Microsoft Copilot Enablement
